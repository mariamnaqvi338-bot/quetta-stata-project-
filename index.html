About the data & methodology

Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS), Labour Force Survey 2020–21, uploaded Quetta Division extract. Weighted counts sum the provided Weights variable; sample counts count respondents. Figures are descriptive estimates, not current population projections.

Derived indicators reproduce the supplied Stata project. Labor force = employed or unemployed. Participation divides labor force by the full selected population, including children when “All ages” is selected; it is not the conventional working-age LFPR. Unemployment rate divides unemployed by labor force. These denominators change with filters.

Respondents not flagged as employed, unemployed, or outside the labor force are shown as unclassified. In the supplied data these are the 0–9 group. Missing or unmapped education and marital categories remain visible as “Unclassified.” Education labels follow the project’s mapping of S4C9 codes 1–7, which has not been independently verified against the PBS codebook.

Small samples are unstable. No survey-design confidence intervals are calculated. The dashboard uses aggregate cells. The separately downloadable Stata dataset is the supplied respondent-level extract and includes survey identifiers; it is not an anonymized dashboard export. Hypothesis tests are quoted from the report, are unweighted, and do not update with filters. Associations do not establish causation.
