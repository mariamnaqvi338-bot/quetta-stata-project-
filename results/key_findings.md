# Key descriptive findings

These results are recomputed from the current dashboard aggregates of the author’s cleaned PBS LFS 2020–21 Quetta extract. They describe the supplied sample and weighted estimates; they do not establish causal effects.

- The analytical sample includes **9,652 respondents**: 4,691 in Quetta, 2,255 in Pishin, and 2,706 in Killa Abdullah.
- **2,121** respondents are classified as employed and **98** as unemployed, producing an unweighted labour-force count of **2,219**.
- The weighted labour force is approximately **881,078**, and weighted unemployment is **3.44%** of that labour force.
- Ages 20–29 account for **31.1%** of weighted employment and **60.8%** of weighted unemployment. These are composition shares, not age-specific employment or unemployment rates.
- **3,143 children aged 0–9** are unclassified by the supplied economic-activity flags. The dashboard retains them rather than assigning them to another status.

## Methodological implications

Clearly specifying denominators avoids conflating shares with rates. Comparing raw records with the cleaned extract establishes provenance but does not independently validate the substantive coding rules. Historical logs preserve analytical context, including errors, rather than proving a clean execution in a new environment. Official coding documentation and survey-design inference remain areas for further work.
